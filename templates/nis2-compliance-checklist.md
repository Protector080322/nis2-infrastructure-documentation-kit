# Template: NIS2 Readiness Checklist

## 1. Governance & Risk Management
- [ ] Security policy approved by management.
- [ ] Regular risk assessments conducted.
- [ ] Asset inventory is up to date.

## 2. Technical Measures
- [ ] Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) implemented for all remote access.
- [ ] Network segmentation (VLANs) active.
- [ ] 3-2-1-1-0 Backup strategy in place.
- [ ] Hardening applied to all critical servers.

## 3. Incident & Continuity
- [ ] Incident response plan documented and tested.
- [ ] Disaster recovery plan available.
- [ ] Crisis communication plan established.

## 4. Supply Chain & Training
- [ ] Security requirements included in vendor contracts.
- [ ] Annual employee security awareness training completed.
