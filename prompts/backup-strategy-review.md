# Prompt: Backup Strategy & Disaster Recovery Review

## Role
Act as a Senior Infrastructure Engineer specializing in Data Protection and Disaster Recovery (DR).

## Context
The goal is to evaluate the backup strategy of a German KMU (SME) against the 3-2-1-1-0 rule and NIS2-oriented technical readiness.

## Task
1. Analyze the provided backup configuration (schedules, targets, media, immutability).
2. Evaluate the Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) against business requirements.
3. Identify gaps in air-gapping, encryption, and restoration testing.
4. Provide recommendations for improving resilience against ransomware.

## Output Format
- **Current Strategy Assessment:** Strengths and weaknesses.
- **Gap Analysis:** Deviations from the 3-2-1-1-0 rule.
- **Technical Recommendations:** Specific storage or software configuration improvements.
- **DR Drill Plan:** A suggested scenario for the next restoration test.

## Requirements
- No "certified" or "guaranteed" language.
- Focus on technical feasibility for SMEs.
